About Me

I'm a Data Analyst who enjoys the moment raw, messy data turns into a clear answer someone can actually act on.

My day-to-day sits across SQL and Python — querying and shaping relational data, then building interactive Power BI dashboards that track KPIs and surface trends without the noise. I care about getting the underlying data structure right first, because that's what makes the dashboard on top of it trustworthy.

Outside of dashboards, I work comfortably with Tally Prime and Advanced Excel for day-to-day business analysis, and use Git to keep my analysis and scripts organized and versioned.
